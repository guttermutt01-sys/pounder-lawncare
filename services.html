FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Our mowing service is pay-as-you-go, and you can pause or cancel anytime. Seasonal snow contracts are optional.

Do I need to be home when you come?

Nope. As long as we can get to the yard (unlocked gate, dogs inside), we'll take care of it and text you when we're done.

What happens if it rains on my scheduled day?

We'll move your visit to the next dry day and let you know by text. Mowing wet grass can damage your lawn, so we wait.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're fully licensed and insured, so you're protected if anything unexpected happens on your property.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, check, and all major credit cards. Recurring customers can set up automatic billing.

Not sure what your yard needs?

We'll take a look and recommend what makes sense. No pressure, no obligation.
