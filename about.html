Our story

It started with one mower and a few neighbors.

Pounder Lawn Care began when our founder started mowing lawns on his street to earn some extra money. Word got around that he showed up on time, did the job right, and actually cared how the yard looked.

Fifteen years later we've grown into a full crew serving hundreds of homes and businesses, but we still run things the same way: show up when we say we will, do great work, and treat every customer like a neighbor.

Jim Lingers Owner & Founder
